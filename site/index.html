Every forge you use, together in one timeline.

Connect personal, work, and community accounts across cloud and self-hosted servers at the same time. Every row carries a corner forge badge so you always know which server and account you are acting on, or filter down to a single forge in one tap.

GitHub

github.com

Sign in with one-tap device flow or personal access tokens. Keep work organizations and personal repositories side-by-side with full Notifications, Pull Requests, Issues, Discussions, project boards, and GitHub Actions support.

GitLab

gitlab.com & self-hosted

Works with both gitlab.com and your own self-hosted GitLab server. Syncs GitLab To-Dos directly into your Inbox, with native Merge Request diffs and CI/CD pipeline logs.

Codeberg

codeberg.org

Support for the community forge, sign in via browser OAuth with PKCE or token, manage issues and pull requests, and browse daily Codeberg Trending.

Forgejo

Self-hosted instances

Connect any self-hosted Forgejo server on your own domain, even behind a private certificate authority or under a path. Browse private and public repositories, review pull requests, and manage repository settings directly from your phone.

Gitea

gitea.com & self-hosted

Works with gitea.com and your own Gitea server, told apart from Forgejo so you get what Gitea offers. Sign in to gitea.com through the browser or to your server with a token, review pull requests, and follow Gitea Actions runs.

Tangled

tangled.org

The forge built on the AT Protocol. Browse repositories, people, and Tangled's own Trending without an account; sign in with your handle and an app password to star and follow. Issues and pull requests aren't there yet: Tangled is moving them to a new kind of record.

SourceHut

git.sr.ht & todo.sr.ht

Sign in with a token to browse git.sr.ht repositories, their tags and files, and people, and to read, comment on, and resolve a repository's todo.sr.ht tickets. Patches by email and builds stay on the web.

One Inbox for every forge and account, sorted by what actually needs you.

Stop checking GitHub notifications, work GitLab To-Dos, and Codeberg in separate browser tabs. Forgeline brings every account into a single Inbox and organizes it around your attention:

  • Needs you first, Everything else grouped by repository: Review requests, direct mentions, assignments, security alerts, and GitLab To-Dos sit right at the top, while watched-repo activity is grouped cleanly by owner and repository below.
  • Dedicated "Yours" view across every server: Switch to the Yours tab to see every pull request waiting for your review, your own open PRs and MRs, and issues assigned to you across GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, Forgejo, and Gitea at a glance.
  • Swipe gestures, batch selection & preloaded threads: Swipe right to mark read, swipe left to mark done, or long-press to batch-triage multiple threads at once. Clear every thread whose pull request or issue is closed or merged in one tap, or act straight from a phone notification. Background checks preload conversations waiting on you so they open instantly.

Discover rising projects and where to contribute, follow your feed, and resume where you left off.

Explore what is gaining traction across the open-source ecosystem and follow activity from developers and repositories you care about:

  • Trending across GitHub, Codeberg, GitLab & Tangled: Browse what is trending today, this week, or this month, mixed into a single page or filtered to one forge at a time. Codeberg and gitlab.com velocity is measured daily by Forgeline, and Tangled's picks come as Tangled made them.
  • Chronological activity feed: See what the people you follow and the repositories you watch or star are doing, releases, announcements, stars, and pushes, in strict chronological order, grouped by day, with duplicate events merged cleanly.
  • "Where you left off" session marker: Both Trending and your Feed automatically mark where your last visit's reading stopped so you never waste time re-reading old items.
  • Topics, first issues & collections: Open a topic to see what else is tagged across forges, find issues maintainers marked for a first contribution in projects you starred or saw trending, and keep collections of repositories from any forge. Search GitHub, Codeberg, and gitlab.com without signing in.

Review PR diffs, merge checks, and CI logs natively.

Other mobile clients give up and kick you out to a mobile web page the moment you tap a changed file or a broken pipeline. Forgeline renders the entire review and debugging loop natively:

  • Line-by-line diffs & reviews in full: Inspect changed files with what reviewers already said under their lines, comment on a line, several, or a whole file, answer and resolve threads, commit suggestions, send a review (approve, comment, or request changes), and merge the ways the repository allows.
  • Jumps straight to the first CI step: For GitHub Actions and GitLab CI/CD pipelines, Forgeline highlights failed jobs and steps with colored, folded job logs, live running steps, re-run/cancel controls, and manual workflow dispatch. Gitea Actions runs show their jobs and steps too.
  • Code browser, history, blame & repo settings: Browse files at any branch or tag, colored by VS Code's own grammars in every language, inspect commit history and line-by-line git blame, read wikis, and manage repository settings.
  • Conversations told in full: Every triage event on an issue or pull request reads as a sentence, from review requests to force-pushes. Open issues from the repository's templates and forms, and on GitHub move them across project boards, and start, answer, and vote on discussions.

Fast, local-first, and strictly private.

Forgeline is built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose around a local SQLite cache and direct-to-forge networking, with zero telemetry or intermediary servers.

<1s

Sub-second local SQLite startup

Opens straight into your cached Inbox, Feed, and Trending lists in about a second and refreshes quietly in the background without blocking your screen with skeleton spinners.

Android Keystore & direct API calls

OAuth tokens and Personal Access Tokens stay encrypted in Android Keystore on your device and connect directly to each forge you use, or to your own server.

Zero trackers, 100% GPL-3.0 FOSS

No analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, no tracking pixels, and no proprietary libraries. Signed APKs are built reproducibly on GitHub Actions.
