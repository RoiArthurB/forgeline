One Inbox for every forge and account, sorted by what actually needs you.

Stop checking GitHub notifications, work GitLab To-Dos, and Codeberg in separate browser tabs. Forgeline brings every account into a single Inbox and organizes it around your attention:

  • Needs you first, Everything else grouped by repository: Review requests, direct mentions, assignments, security alerts, and GitLab To-Dos sit right at the top, while watched-repo activity is grouped cleanly by owner and repository below.
  • Dedicated "Yours" view across every server: Switch to the Yours tab to see every pull request waiting for your review, your own open PRs and MRs, and issues assigned to you across GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, and Forgejo at a glance.
  • Swipe gestures, batch selection & preloaded threads: Swipe right to mark read, swipe left to mark done, or long-press to batch-triage multiple threads at once. Background checks preload conversations waiting on you so they open instantly.

Review PR diffs, merge checks, and CI logs natively.

Other mobile clients give up and kick you out to a mobile web page the moment you tap a changed file or a broken pipeline. Forgeline renders the entire review and debugging loop natively:

  • Line-by-line diffs & inline reviews: Inspect changed files, tap any line to comment on it, send a review (approve, comment, or request changes), and merge directly from the app.
  • Jumps straight to the first CI step: For GitHub Actions and GitLab CI/CD pipelines, Forgeline highlights failed jobs and steps with colored, folded job logs, live running steps, re-run/cancel controls, and manual workflow dispatch.
  • Code browser, history, blame & repo settings: Browse syntax-highlighted files at any branch or tag, inspect commit history and line-by-line git blame, and manage repository settings.

Fast, local-first, and strictly private.

Forgeline is built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose around a local SQLite cache and direct-to-forge networking, with zero telemetry or intermediary servers.

<1s

Sub-second local SQLite startup

Opens straight into your cached Inbox, Feed, and Trending lists in about a second and refreshes quietly in the background without blocking your screen with skeleton spinners.

Android Keystore & direct API calls

OAuth tokens and Personal Access Tokens stay encrypted in Android Keystore on your device and connect directly to GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, and your Forgejo server.

Zero trackers, 100% GPL-3.0 FOSS

No analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, no tracking pixels, and no proprietary libraries. Signed APKs are built reproducibly on GitHub Actions.
