Forgeline now speaks GitLab, and you can run your issues from the app: open, close, label, assign and more. The Inbox is quicker to clear, long conversations are quick to cross, and each release has its own page. It needs Android 12 or later.

**GitLab is new in this release and has been checked against gitlab.com, not yet lived with on a phone.** If something looks wrong there, please open an issue.

## Highlights

- **gitlab.com.** Sign in with a personal access token (scopes `api` and `read_user`), then browse projects, read and write on issues and merge requests, follow pipelines and their logs, see your to-do list in the Inbox and your projects' activity in the Feed. Trending rows from gitlab.com now open in the app. Self-hosted GitLab isn't supported yet.
- **Open and manage issues.** A "New issue" action on every repository. From a conversation: close and reopen, and a Manage sheet with labels, assignees, milestone, close reasons, lock, pin, duplicate, transfer and delete. It offers only what the forge can do and your role allows.
- **Long conversations.** Two floating buttons take you to the top and to the latest message, loading whatever is left on the way. A notification you haven't read opens at the first message since you last read it (on GitHub; at the latest message elsewhere).
- **A page for each release.** Its notes, the files it ships with their size and download count, and the source at its tag. It opens from the Releases tab, the Feed and release links.

## Issues and pull requests

- The Issues and Pull requests tabs list open or closed ones, search them by word, and show pinned issues first.
- Timelines now tell of locks, pins, assignments, milestones, transfers and conversions to a discussion. A locked conversation no longer offers a comment box you couldn't use.
- Forgejo and Codeberg: due date with a calendar, time tracker, and dependencies between issues.
- Pull request titles and repository previews in the Feed match whatever the casing of the repository's name.

## Inbox

- **Swipe a repository's heading** to mark every notification under it as done, with one Undo for the lot.
- **Codeberg and Forgejo: "Done" now stays done.** A thread you cleared came back at the next sync, or failed with "Couldn't update the notification". Both causes are fixed.

## Sign-in

- A sign-in that was renewed in the background is no longer lost when you leave the screen that asked for it. This could leave a Codeberg account unable to sync until you signed in again.
- When a forge ends your sign-in, the You tab says so and offers to sign in again, instead of the Inbox and Feed silently staying empty.

## Faster

- Markdown already read is kept, so scrolling back through a conversation or a README doesn't read it again.
- The lists the app keeps on the phone are indexed by what they are sorted on.
- On GitHub, the rest of a long conversation loads all at once instead of page after page.

## Known limitations

- GitLab: gitlab.com only, token sign-in only. Marking a to-do as read doesn't last (GitLab only knows pending and done). Label and milestone changes don't show in timelines, the time tracker's timer can't be started, and comments need an account to be read.
- Comments can't be edited, deleted or previewed, and there is no reply-with-quote.
- Issue and pull request lists stop at their first page.
- Images inside private repositories' READMEs don't load.
- No pull request diffs yet.
- Tablets get the phone layout, centred.

No backend, no analytics, no trackers: the app talks directly to your forges. Forgeline is free software under the GPL-3.0.
