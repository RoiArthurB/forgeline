Forgeline can now write, not only read: you can comment on issues and pull requests. It also reaches your private repositories on GitHub, shows Trending from three forges to everyone, and is faster and safer underneath. It needs Android 12 or later.

**After updating, sign in to GitHub again** (You tab > "Sign in to GitHub again") to open your private repositories. Until you do, your sign-in keeps working on public ones only.

## Highlights

- **Comment on issues and pull requests.** A conversation now ends with a box for your reply, on GitHub, Codeberg and any Forgejo server. It is plain Markdown. A comment that can't be sent stays written and says why, and a draft survives leaving the app.
- **Private repositories on GitHub.** Forgeline used to ask GitHub for public repositories only. It now asks for private ones too, so you can open them, comment, star and start workflows there. GitHub's consent screen calls this "full control of private repositories": it is the narrowest permission GitHub has that reads a private repository, and Forgeline never writes code.
- **Starred repositories in your Feed.** New releases from the repositories you starred now show up, on every forge, and on GitHub their Announcements discussions too. Pre-releases are marked, and you choose what shows in Settings.
- **Trending from Codeberg and gitlab.com, for everyone.** Both are measured once a day and mixed into the page, signed in or not. GitLab rows open in the browser for now.

## Trending

- gitlab.com has no trending page any more, so its projects are measured daily, like Codeberg's. Its weekly and monthly lists are complete from the first day.
- A forge that gained only a handful of stars no longer takes the top of the mixed page: short lists and small totals are weighed down.

## Inbox and Feed

- Inbox headings show the forge's logo next to the owner and the repository when you use several forges.
- Conversations waiting on you are loaded ahead even once read, so reopening them is instant.
- Pulling to refresh the Feed stops as soon as the first forge answers, instead of waiting for the slowest.
- The Codeberg Inbox no longer fails when Codeberg's own searches are slow.

## Faster

- Avatars download at the size they are shown: a fifth of the data on GitHub.
- Pictures load many at a time instead of five.
- Lists are built off the main thread, and the Feed no longer rebuilds itself for every preview that arrives while you scroll.
- The app ships a fresh startup profile recorded from the current screens.

## Safer

- **Signing out deletes what was kept.** Notifications, Feed, conversations and repositories read with the account are removed from the phone. Data left behind by earlier versions is cleaned up at launch.
- **No crash on an unexpected answer.** A forge answering with a web page or incomplete data now shows an error instead of closing the app. On "another server", the sign-in screen says when the address isn't a forge.
- **Your place is kept across updates.** Reading marks, threads marked done on Codeberg and Trending measured on your phone moved to storage that updates no longer wipe.
- Two refreshes at once can no longer spend the same Codeberg sign-in renewal.

## Known limitations

- GitLab is Trending only: no sign-in, and its projects open in the browser.
- Comments can't be edited, deleted or previewed, and there is no reply-with-quote.
- Images inside private repositories' READMEs don't load.
- A GitHub organisation that restricts apps hides its private repositories until an owner approves Forgeline; a personal access token works around it.
- No pull request diffs yet.
- Tablets get the phone layout, centred.
- Some Actions messages still say "GitHub" on Forgejo runs.

No backend, no analytics, no trackers: the app talks directly to your forges. Forgeline is free software under the GPL-3.0.
