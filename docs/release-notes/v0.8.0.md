Forgeline is redrawn, its Feed is richer, and you can find any of your repositories from the You tab. It needs Android 12 or later.

**Nothing in this release has been tried on a phone yet** (everything is checked in tests, and several forge answers against the real APIs). If something looks wrong, please open an issue.

## Highlights

- **A new look: "Ink Compact".** An ink header band with text tabs and a marigold underline, Geist for all text, compact rows led by big icon tiles, and each row's forge as a small badge on its tile (shown once you are signed in to more than one). Settings → Appearance → Header offers Ink, True black or White. Comments are sent with a send arrow. The launcher icon and splash screen follow the same ink and marigold.
- **A Feed with pictures.** Releases show their project's logo, the version with how big a step it is (patch, minor, major, pre-release), the project's own picture, the start of the release notes and reactions. Pull requests show their size, issues their labels and comments. Pictures can be switched off in Settings → Feed. New repositories made by people you follow and your organisations are back in the Feed.
- **The You tab finds your repositories.** Every repository your accounts reach (yours, your organisations', and ones shared with you), most recently pushed first, with a field to find one and a row of owners to narrow the list: you, Shared, then each organisation. Long-press an owner to open their page.
- **Profiles.** Private repositories on your own page and your organisations'. Email, social accounts and chat links with their logos. The profile README, in a tab of its own. A colour bar of the languages on each repository.
- **Pull requests in full.** What changes, commits and checks, reviewing, merging, opening one, file history and blame; a check's report opens in the app.
- **Repository settings** on all three forges: About, features, merging, Pages and a danger zone (visibility, archive, rename, delete), with Undo for switches.
- **An Inbox that follows the forge's site**, with multi-select, "unread again", your own work as a filter, and a home-screen widget. French is available, and links to your own servers open in the app.

## Fixes

- GitLab profiles now show their bio, website and company.
- The review button on a pull request no longer hides behind the navigation bar.
- A running job no longer shows a frozen spinner arc with animations off, and job rows line up with their heading.

## Known limitations

- Pictures in private repositories don't load in the code browser.
- A GitHub organisation's profile can't show social accounts: GitHub's API has none.
- An organisation's new repository in the Feed is signed by the organisation, not by the member who made it, and can take up to 30 minutes to appear.
- Discussions can't be written in yet, and a wiki opens in the browser.
- Marking a GitLab to-do as read doesn't last (GitLab only knows pending and done).
- Tablets get the phone layout, centred.

No backend, no analytics, no trackers: the app talks directly to your forges. Forgeline is free software under the GPL-3.0.
