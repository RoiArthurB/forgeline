Forgeline now works with more than GitHub: Codeberg and any self-hosted Forgejo server sit in the same Inbox, Feed, Trending and search, with several accounts at once. It needs Android 12 or later.

## Highlights

- **Codeberg and Forgejo, everywhere.** Sign in to Codeberg (one-tap) or to any Forgejo server (with a token), next to GitHub. The Inbox, Feed, Trending, search, repositories, conversations, profiles and Actions all work on every forge you signed in to.
- **Always clear which forge.** Wherever a list mixes forges, each row says "GitHub", "Codeberg" or the server's host beside its logo, so two repositories with the same name are never confusing.
- **Opens on what fits.** Signed in, Forgeline opens on your Inbox. Signed in nowhere, it opens on Trending, with a "Browse Trending" button on the signed-out Inbox and Feed.
- **Every tab is labelled.** The navigation bar shows a name under each icon.
- **Faster from far away.** Accounts refresh in parallel, the Inbox no longer waits for work you can't see, and requests to one forge run 16 at a time instead of 5.

## Multiple forges

- **One list, or one forge.** Trending, search and the Inbox can show every forge mixed, or one forge alone: a small "All forges" or "All accounts" pill in the header opens the choice.
- **Several accounts.** Every signed-in account is listed on the You page. With more than one, the Inbox can split per account.
- **Codeberg Trending.** Codeberg has no Trending page, so it is measured daily and mixed into yours by each repository's share of its forge's stars. It fills in over time: today's list from tomorrow, the week complete by 2026-10-07, the month by 2026-10-30. Self-hosted servers can be measured on your phone (Settings > Trending, off by default).
- **Actions on Codeberg.** Runs, jobs and logs, starting a workflow, and cancelling a run. Codeberg has no re-run, and its runs open in the browser from a link.
- **Stars, follows and runs ask for an account on the item's own forge.**
- Settings is now split into pages.

## Better with large text, landscape and screen readers

- At the largest system font, rows, switches and ranks no longer split words or hide names, and a strip of tabs scrolls to the one you chose.
- In landscape, nothing sits under the navigation bar or a camera cutout, and search results and the token field stay above the keyboard.
- Screen readers hear "Unread" on unread notifications, repository, issue, profile and job titles as headings, and whether a repository is starred.
- Empty, error and search messages name the forge they are about, and GitHub's search syntax is only suggested when searching GitHub.
- Log colors and the dark theme's snackbar buttons meet contrast.

## Fixes

- Some pull request titles in the Feed stayed blank; they now load in the background and retry.
- The same repository trending on two forges no longer crashes Trending.
- Runs on older Forgejo servers that can't list a run's jobs now open.

## Known limitations

- GitLab is still to come.
- No pull request diffs, commenting or editing yet.
- Tablets get the phone layout, centred; there is no tablet-specific layout.
- Some Actions messages still say "GitHub" on Forgejo runs.

No backend, no analytics, no trackers: the app talks directly to your forges. Forgeline is free software under the GPL-3.0.
