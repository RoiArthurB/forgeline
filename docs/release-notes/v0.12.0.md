Forgeline now tells a conversation's whole story: every triage event on GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, Forgejo and Gitea reads as a sentence, from project boards to force-pushes. SourceHut repositories gain their tickets, Settings gains an Accessibility page, and the app is getting ready for F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid. It needs Android 12 or later.

## Highlights

- **Every triage event, as a sentence.** A conversation's timeline used to keep comments, reviews, labels and a few more. It now also tells review requests, pushes and force-pushes, drafts, duplicates, sub-issues and parents, dependencies, a new target branch, auto-merge and merge queues or trains, deployments, pinned and deleted comments, moves and clones, time spent and estimates, and the commits and conversations that mention it, on GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, Forgejo and Gitea, each as far as its forge tells. An event that names another conversation opens it.
- **GitHub project boards.** An issue or pull request lists the boards it is on, with its column on each; if you may update a board, tap it to move the conversation to another column, or off it. This needs the project scope, which sign-in now asks: sign in to GitHub again to see boards.
- **GitLab's labels and milestones** show in a conversation's timeline, with its weights and iterations: GitLab keeps them apart from its notes, and they were missing.
- **SourceHut tickets.** A git.sr.ht repository whose owner keeps a todo.sr.ht tracker of the same name has an Issues tab. Read a ticket as a conversation; signed in, comment, open, edit and subscribe, and as the tracker allows label, assign, resolve with a reason, reopen and delete. todo.sr.ht links open in the app. Your token needs todo.sr.ht access too.
- **Accessibility page.** Settings › Accessibility gathers text size (smaller, as on the phone by default, larger or largest), the phone's own font instead of Geist (code stays monospace), and roomy links: a finger's height for each link that sits close to another, off by default.
- **Sign-in starts at a list of every forge**, each with its logo and where it is, instead of a row of tabs that ran off the screen.
- **Animated GIFs** play in READMEs and comments, and so do animated WebP pictures.
- **Tangled people have their pictures**, and a Tangled profile shows its description, location and links.

## Fixes

- A discussion's comment and reply boxes stayed under the keyboard; the tab bar rose onto the keyboard and covered them.
- A message saying a comment didn't go through came up under the keyboard.
- Line numbers beside code broke over two lines at the largest text sizes.
- A repository's topics wrapped into rows of small tags where a near miss opened the one beside it: they now sit on one line that scrolls.
- TalkBack didn't say that a repository's owner and website, a release's author, a Feed picture, a page's repository and authors' names open something.
- A discussion's tags lost their box on its band, and its pictures were round instead of rounded squares.

## Known limitations

- GitHub: a sign-in made before this version shows no project boards, and its events say "a project", until you sign in again.
- Tangled: no issues, pull requests or releases yet (Tangled is moving them to a new kind of record), no CI, Feed, Inbox or search, and sign-in is by app password, not through the browser.
- SourceHut: no patches (lists.sr.ht) or builds, no Feed, Inbox or Trending, tickets only from the tracker named after the repository, and only sr.ht itself, not a server of your own.
- A self-hosted Gitea signs in with a token: only gitea.com has Forgeline's application.
- On Codeberg, a remark on several lines or a whole file, viewed files, hidden comments, sub-issues and issue types aren't offered (Forgejo's API has none of them), a suggestion can't be committed, and a remark you take back stays.
- Marking a GitLab to-do as read doesn't last (GitLab only knows pending and done).

No backend, no analytics, no trackers: the app talks directly to your forges. Forgeline is free software under the GPL-3.0.
