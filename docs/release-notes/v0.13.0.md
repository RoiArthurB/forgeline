Forgeline now reads and writes GitHub discussions end to end: start one, comment, reply, vote, react, follow, and open one straight from its notification. Search works signed out on GitHub, Codeberg and gitlab.com, you choose which account acts on each forge, and pull requests show what reviewers already said on the change. It needs Android 12 or later.

## Highlights

- **GitHub discussions, in full.** Start a discussion from a repository's Discussions tab; comment and reply with references, saved replies and a preview, as in conversations; rewrite or delete your own comments, replies and the discussion itself; upvote and react; follow or stop following. A long discussion loads every comment and reply, and an unread one opens at what is new. The Inbox says whether a discussion is open, answered or closed, and reads one waiting on you ahead so its page opens at once. A discussion's notification opens the discussion, not its repository.
- **Search signed out.** GitHub, Codeberg and gitlab.com can be searched without an account. Codeberg searches repositories only for people signed in to it, and Forgeline says so and offers to sign in.
- **Choose which account acts on a forge.** With several accounts on one forge, pick the one that answers, stars and reads private pictures; a thread is answered as the account it came to. Unsent drafts survive signing out of one account while another remains.
- **Reviews on the change.** A pull request's change shows what reviewers already said, under the lines it's about. A review's remarks stay on their lines until it's sent, and tapping one rewrites it. A review thread opens its file in the change.
- **Notifications.** Mark a thread read or done from its phone notification; opening a thread from a notification or the widget marks it read.
- **Gitea Actions.** Runs and jobs with their steps, and runs listed signed out on gitea.com.
- **Copy and select.** Select and copy text in READMEs, files and comments, and copy a code block whole. A link to a file opens it on the lines it names, marked.
- **Private servers.** Sign in to self-hosted servers behind a private certificate authority or installed under a path.
- **Better links.** A profile README's links to repositories, conversations and people open in the app; a file leads on to its repository, history and blame; Back goes up a folder in the code.

## Fixes

- SourceHut and Tangled no longer offer what they can't do: reactions, comment edits, History, repository Settings, the Starred tab, or stars shown as 0; the Inbox and Yours say plainly that they keep no notifications.
- A SourceHut login reads `~name`, not `@~name`.
- A GitLab to-do you opened stays read until something new happens on it.
- The Feed keeps paging past activity Settings hides, and Trending remembers where its browse stopped.
- Searching Codeberg and gitlab.com continues after signing in to GitHub.
- A failed search says why when the other forges found nothing.
- Large text: the merge, review and remark sheets scroll so their button stays in reach; the contribution graph's months stay clear of its squares; a discussion answer's author stays named.
- Markdown links take the weight of the words around them, and a forge's labels are drawn as tags.
- French: "relecture" for a review throughout, the All filter named as its tab, and a profile's email and website named in the reader's language.
- First issues are looked up again after signing in or out, and found on gitlab.com signed out.

## Known limitations

- Discussions exist on GitHub only: no other supported forge has a discussions API.
- Tangled: no issues, pull requests or releases yet, no CI, Feed, Inbox or search, and sign-in is by app password.
- SourceHut: no patches or builds, no Feed, Inbox or Trending, tickets only from the tracker named after the repository, and only sr.ht itself.
- A self-hosted Gitea signs in with a token: only gitea.com has Forgeline's application.
- On Codeberg, a remark on several lines or a whole file, viewed files, hidden comments, sub-issues and issue types aren't offered, a suggestion can't be committed, and a remark you take back stays.
- Marking a GitLab to-do as read doesn't last across a refresh by GitLab (it only knows pending and done).

No backend, no analytics, no trackers: the app talks directly to your forges. Forgeline is free software under the GPL-3.0.
