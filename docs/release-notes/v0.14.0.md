Forgeline now lets you set up your own profile and the organizations you own from the app, on every forge, as far as each one's API allows. Tangled gets issues and pull requests, the Inbox picks many threads with one drag, the Feed opens up to date and shows a merged pull request once, and Trending ranks by growth. It needs Android 12 or later.

## Highlights

- **Your profile and your organizations, from the app.** A gear on your own profile, and on an organization (a GitLab group) you own, opens what its forge lets you change: your name, bio, pronouns, company, location, website, links and what your profile hides; an organization's texts, visibility and what its members may do. On GitHub and GitLab, your status too (an emoji, a message, busy, and when it clears).
- **Members, invitations and blocks.** See who is in an organization and what each may do; change their role, remove them, or invite someone by username or email (on Forgejo and Gitea, add them to a team). A Blocked tab lists who you or an organization block, to unblock them or block someone by name. On GitHub, limit who may comment and open issues and pull requests in your public repositories for a while.
- **Pictures.** Change your picture, or an organization's, with the phone's photo picker: on Forgejo, Gitea, GitLab, Tangled and SourceHut.
- **An organization's danger zone.** Rename an organization (on Forgejo and Gitea, and a GitLab group's path) or delete it once its name is written out, on a red-tinted page like a repository's, which is now tinted too.
- **Tangled issues and pull requests.** List, read, open, comment and react, close and reopen, label and assign; a pull request's files (from forks too), commits and checks; history, blame, search, the Feed, and links that open in the app. Pin up to six repositories to your Tangled profile.
- **The Inbox, faster to sort.** Long-press a thread and keep your finger down: every thread you draw over is picked, and the list scrolls at its edges. Either swipe can unsubscribe, and a phone notification can carry an Unsubscribe button (Settings > Inbox).
- **The Feed, up to date and less repetitive.** It loads in the background (every hour by default, or never), with the previews of its first rows, so it opens on what is new. A pull request or issue opened and since merged or closed shows once. It keeps a month of activity, and says who joined a repository.
- **Trending by growth.** Repositories are ranked by how fast they grow against their forge's usual pace, and gitea.com's Trending joins everyone's page.
- **Search sorted.** Sort results by best match, stars, newest or last update; a forge that can't sort says so.
- **Pull requests.** Each commit says whether its checks passed, and line remarks sit under the review that made them.
- **README pictures.** A logo beside a row of badges, and a picture linked to itself or to a larger copy, now open larger with a tap.

## Fixes

- Closed Tangled pull requests list again, the Tangled Feed reads signed in as Tangled now asks, and a pull request from a fork shows its files.
- Codeberg's daily Trending waits out rate limiting and keeps its list when a page is slow.
- Trending no longer shows a "where you left off" mark.
- Dependency updates: Android Gradle plugin 9.5.2, kotlinx-serialization 1.13.0, Gson 2.15.0, KSP 2.3.14, ozone 0.3.5 and the testing libraries.

## Before you use it

- **Sign in to GitHub again** to change your profile or an organization's settings: Forgeline now asks for the `user` and `admin:org` permissions, which earlier sign-ins don't have. Everything else keeps working until then, and the settings say so.
- A Codeberg or Forgejo access token needs `write:user` and `write:organization` for these settings; a SourceHut token needs meta.sr.ht's profile scope, read and write.

## Known limitations

- GitLab lets only its administrators change a profile's name, bio, location or links, or block someone, through its API: there, your status, picture and the website's diff preferences.
- GitHub has no API for a profile or organization picture, for renaming an organization, or for pinned repositories.
- Not offered yet: creating or editing Forgejo teams, GitHub organization rulesets and Actions permissions, organization webhooks and labels.
- Discussions exist on GitHub only: no other supported forge has a discussions API.
- Tangled: no releases, no Inbox and no organizations, and sign-in is by app password.
- SourceHut: no patches or builds, no Feed, Inbox or Trending, tickets only from the tracker named after the repository, and only sr.ht itself.
- A self-hosted Gitea signs in with a token: only gitea.com has Forgeline's application.
- On Codeberg, a remark on several lines or a whole file, viewed files, hidden comments, sub-issues and issue types aren't offered, a suggestion can't be committed, and a remark you take back stays.
- Marking a GitLab to-do as read doesn't last across a refresh by GitLab (it only knows pending and done).

No backend, no analytics, no trackers: the app talks directly to your forges. Forgeline is free software under the GPL-3.0.
