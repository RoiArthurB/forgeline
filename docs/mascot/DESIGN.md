# Forgeline Mascot Guidelines: Forgeline

This document defines the character design, anatomy, and usage rules for the Forgeline mascot. She bridges the classic 1920s rubber hose animation aesthetic with Forgeline's "Friendly Type" design system, resulting in a soft, bouncy, and highly recognizable brand ambassador.

## 1. Core Identity & Anatomy

* **The Body:** A squishy, stylized anvil. Despite being made of "metal," she is soft and doughy, with heavily rounded corners that echo Forgeline's UI shape language (like the 28dp header fields and 24dp pill shapes). There are absolutely no sharp points or rigid straight lines.
* **The Face:**
* **Eyes:** Classic 1920s "pie-cut" style (tall black ovals with a triangular slice removed for the catchlight). She features prominent, swooping eyelashes to clearly read as feminine.
* **Expression:** A small, cute button nose and a highly expressive, elastic mouth. She defaults to a warm, welcoming smile or a playful wink.
* **The Limbs:** Pure "noodle" anatomy. Her arms and legs are solid black tubes of consistent thickness. She has no elbows, wrists, knees, or ankles. Limbs should curve in sweeping, fluid arcs.
* **The Extremities:**
* **Hands:** Plump, oversized white gloves with rolled cuffs and the classic three black darts on the back.
* **Feet:** Rounded, chunky white work shoes with a subtle feminine heel.

## 2. The Color Palette

The mascot strictly follows Forgeline's monochrome + single accent rule. She uses only three colors mapped directly to the app's design tokens:
* **Ink (`#1E1B2E`):** Used for all linework, the noodle limbs, the pie-cut eyes, and the mouth interior. It provides high contrast without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
* **Ground/White (`#FAFAFB` or pure `#FFFFFF`):** Used for the gloves, the shoes, the eyes, and as a base for the anvil's soft grey shading.
* **The Ember Accent (`#B83A17`):** Used **exclusively** for her tied neck scarf. The scarf is tied in a classic scout/sailor knot. This maps directly to the app's use of Ember for momentum and selection, ensuring the mascot visually belongs inside the app.

## 3. Posing & Character Acting

* **Elasticity:** Because she lacks joints, her poses should rely on sweeping curves (C-curves and S-curves).
* **Weight:** Her anvil body acts as a heavy, grounded anchor, while her limbs and face are weightless and highly expressive.
* **Props:** When interacting with code forge concepts (like holding a commit node, wielding a blacksmith hammer, or looking at a pull request), the props should share her rounded, soft-cornered geometry.

## 4. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

* **Do** keep the linework bold and consistent in thickness to ensure she reads well at tiny sizes (like an Android launcher icon).
* **Do** use the Ember scarf as the single focal point of color.
* **Do** emphasize a friendly, "discovery-first" personality—she should look helpful and comfy, never aggressive or gritty.

### Don't:

* **Don't** add joints. No elbows or knees ever.
* **Don't** use gradients, drop shadows, or realistic metallic rendering. Stick to flat colors or simple, high-contrast comic shading (like stippling or halftones).
* **Don't** dress her in full clothes (no aprons, hats, or pants). The white gloves, white shoes, and Ember scarf are her complete outfit.
* **Don't** introduce other colors (like blue, green, or yellow). Stick to the Ink, White, and Ember palette.
